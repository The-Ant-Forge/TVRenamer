A small follow-up to v1.0.397.

## Matching tables open in alphabetical order

The **Overrides** and **Disambiguations** tables in Preferences now open sorted alphabetically by their first column (**Extracted show** and **Query string** respectively), with the sort arrow shown on that column so the heading matches what is on screen. Previously they appeared in whatever order the entries happened to be saved in, and sorting was only available by clicking a column heading.

Clicking a heading still sorts by that column, and clicking it again still reverses the order. A newly added entry still appears at the top, so you can see what you just added; it takes its alphabetical place the next time you open Preferences.
